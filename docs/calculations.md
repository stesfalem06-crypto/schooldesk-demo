# How marks, rank, fees and absence are worked out

## Marks

Continuous assessment is 60%. It has six parts: class work, quizzes, tests, assignments, participation and homework. Their weights must add up to 60. The school can set the weights and lock them, or a teacher can set them for one subject.

Each part is split in half. The first half is the mid result, out of 30. The second half is the rest of the 60.

For one half of one part, the score is points earned divided by points possible, times half of that part's weight. Marks that have not been entered yet are left out. A zero that was entered counts.

The semester exam is one mark, scaled to 40.

The subject total is the continuous mark plus the exam, out of 100. Numbers are rounded to two decimal places. A subject is passed only when the total is above 50. A total of 50.00 is not a pass.

## Average and rank

The average is the sum of the subject totals divided by the number of subjects the student takes, rounded to two decimal places.

Students are ranked by that average. If two averages are the same they share the rank and the next rank is skipped: 1, 2, 2, 4. Students who have left, and students with no subjects, are not ranked.

## Promoted or failed

A student is promoted only when the average is above 50 and the number of failed subjects is below the limit for that level. The limits are set separately for elementary, junior and high school. The starting limits are 2, 3 and 3.

An average of 50.00 is failed. A student can also be failed by too many failed subjects even when the average is above 50.

Students who have left are counted as dropouts, not as promoted or failed.

## Fees

The yearly fee is paid once. There is no discount. The homeroom teacher records each amount paid. Remaining is the yearly fee minus the amount paid. A payment that would go past the fee is refused.

## Absence

Absent days are school days in the month. Periods missed are the periods that teacher had on those weekdays, taken from the timetable. If the school is not using the timetable, the weekly numbers entered for that teacher are used instead.

Make-up periods are subtracted from the periods missed. The result is not allowed to go below zero. That result is the periods lost.

Absenteeism per week is absent days divided by the number of school weeks in the month. Periods lost per week uses the same weeks. The percentage lost is periods lost divided by the periods scheduled in the month.
