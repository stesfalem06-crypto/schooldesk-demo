# Director

You can see everything the admin sees: school totals, fees, money, staff, absence and the timetable. You do not change the yearly fee. That stays with the admin.

## Meetings

Tap **Meetings**. Enter the date, a title, who came, and notes if you want them. The notes can be left empty.

## Report

Tap **Report**. Tick the parts you want: school, results, fees, money, staff, absence, meetings.

Then tap **Save PDF**, **Save Word** or **Save PowerPoint**. The file is made on the school computer. It includes charts and the footer on every page or slide.

The semester stats for each class, and one for the whole school, are also made when the admin closes the semester, or on the semester end date set in Settings.
